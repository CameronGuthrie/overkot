Widgets

Arrange your overlay and configure individual widgets.

Layout

Add widgets to put their icons on the bar. Click an icon to open or hide its panel.

Hold Shift to arrange widgets on the alignment grid. Drag a panel away from the bar to float it, or onto the highlighted bar to dock it again. Release Shift to snap floating panels to the grid.

Widget sizes

Hold Shift and drag a widget’s lower-right corner to resize its width and height. Hold Alt as well for 1 px precision.

Sizes are saved for closed widgets too. Panels are kept inside the current screen. A fixed height keeps the content scrollable. Daily Brief and Market resize in their own windows. Text, spacing and icon sizes are in Appearance.

Saved layouts

Name and save your bar position, widget choices, open panels, docking, positions and sizes. Apply a layout whenever you want to switch arrangements.

Choose a saved layout to restore when Overkot starts, or keep your last used arrangement. Changing this choice does not apply a layout now.

No layouts saved yet. Arrange your widgets, enter a name, then save your current layout.

Export a layout to share it or keep a backup. Import adds a saved layout; choose Apply when you are ready. Account details, game paths and appearance presets stay separate.
