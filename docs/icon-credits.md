# Interface icon credits

Overkot bundles a small SVG subset from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/). The library is free for personal and commercial use under the [ISC licence](https://lucide.dev/license); its Feather-derived subset carries Cole Bemis's MIT licence. Both permit modification and distribution when their notices are retained. The exact combined upstream notice ships in [assets/icons/lucide/LICENSE](../assets/icons/lucide/LICENSE) and appears in Settings → About.

Upstream: [lucide-icons/lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide), pinned commit [`742ea78395b4122e13fd46743caba5140f47fb66`](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/742ea78395b4122e13fd46743caba5140f47fb66), verified 20 November 2026. [The local manifest](../assets/icons/lucide/manifest.json) records the selected icons, mappings and SHA-256 digests of their LF-normalised upstream sources.

Each upstream SVG shape is retained once, as its original path, circle, rectangle or other primitive. Independent relative paths are never concatenated. Authored semantic groups assign a primary colour and, where useful, an accent; only purposeful details such as rain or palette pigments use the third channel. A simple shirt, check, chevron or market sack remains a coherent single-colour symbol.

Motion follows each icon's meaning: clock hands turn around the dial centre, complete tools strike, the globe's meridian narrows and pens make a short writing stroke. The weather icon keeps its solar arc and cloud fixed while rays turn only six degrees and rain falls a short distance. The action occupies the first half of a cycle, followed by a calm pause. The refresh symbol has a local fit for its full rotation; the filled market sack has a local optical fit to balance surrounding outlines. All other glyphs retain their original viewBox geometry and scale. [The authored role and motion catalogue](../scripts/icon-semantics.js) records every component and origin; [the motion stylesheet](../scripts/icon-motion.css) defines its bounded movement.

There is no external icon player, CDN or network request. Generic controls, the widget picker, dock and appearance previews use the same family. Overkot's existing market sack, application branding and Soulframe artwork retain their existing ownership; the Lucide licence does not grant rights to those separate assets.

In Settings → Appearance → Animated icons, choose Hover, Loop or Off; choose Theme or Custom colours; set the three available colour channels and animation speed. Each icon uses only the channels its natural components need, usually one or two. These fields support global defaults, widget overrides, undo, live preview, Apply/Cancel, named appearance presets and appearance import/export. The preview plays automatically while live controls follow the chosen mode. Motion Off and respected system reduced motion stop decorative icon animations; hidden windows pause them. High Contrast retains its readable theme palette.

Regenerate the committed catalogues without network access using `node scripts/build-ui-icons.js`. Keep the source SVGs and exact licence together when updating the pinned subset. `test/uiIcons.test.js` checks source integrity, offline asset paths, reproducible generation, dynamic control hydration and bundled notices.

For native optical and motion review, run `node scripts/check-icon-review.js` (also with `--speed=50` or `--speed=200`). It writes an interactive contact sheet, static and synchronized motion frames at 16/20/32 px, and geometry/motion reports under ignored `output/icon-review-*`. Every symbol must have working component motion and remain inside its control padding. `node scripts/check-settings.js --source` additionally verifies live palette changes, whole-control hover, isolated keyboard focus, Off, reduced motion and persisted settings in a temporary app profile. Omit `--source` after a local build to repeat against the packaged assets.

Lordicon and Lottie assets are not bundled. [Lordicon's free licence](https://lordicon.com/docs/license/free) currently allows free icons in commercial projects with credit, but also includes redistribution restrictions. Lucide's permissive source licence provides a clear fit for a locally packaged application and its source repository.
